# AWS re:Post Private

## Purpose

Private knowledge-base and forum for customers on Enterprise Support or Enterprise On-Ramp plans. Centralises curated AWS technical content and private team discussion.

## Trade-offs

- Gated behind Enterprise Support / Enterprise On-Ramp. Smaller
  estates can't access it. The feature is part of the support
  package, not a standalone purchase.
- Internal knowledge tends to live wherever the team already
  uses (Confluence, Notion, internal wiki). re:Post Private's
  forum-shape often duplicates that effort rather than
  centralising it.
- Useful when AWS Account Team needs a private surface to share
  account-specific guidance; less useful as the primary
  knowledge platform.
