# Dynamics 365 Intelligent Order Management

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Intelligent Order Management is the AI-driven order orchestration platform in Dynamics 365 that routes, fulfils, and tracks orders across multiple order sources, fulfilment systems, and logistics providers based on configurable business rules and ML-powered optimisation. It is designed for omnichannel retailers and manufacturers that receive orders from multiple channels (ERP, e-commerce, EDI) and need a centralised orchestration layer to decide which fulfilment node (warehouse, store, drop-ship partner) handles each order at lowest cost and highest service level.
