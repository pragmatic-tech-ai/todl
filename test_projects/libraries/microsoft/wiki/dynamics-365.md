# Dynamics 365

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 is Microsoft's portfolio of cloud-based business applications covering CRM (Sales, Customer Service, Contact Center, Customer Insights) and ERP (Finance, Supply Chain Management, Commerce, Human Resources, Project Operations). The applications share a common data platform (Dataverse), integration with Microsoft 365, and Copilot-embedded AI assistance. Dynamics 365 extends or replaces earlier on-premises Dynamics CRM and Dynamics AX/NAV deployments.
