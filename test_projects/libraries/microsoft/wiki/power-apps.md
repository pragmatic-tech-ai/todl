# Power Apps

## Purpose

Power Apps is the low-code application development platform in Microsoft Power Platform for building canvas apps (freeform, pixel-positioned UI), model-driven apps (data-schema-driven forms and views over Dataverse), and pages (portal-facing web experiences via Power Pages). It allows business users and fusion teams to build custom applications connected to hundreds of data sources without writing traditional application code, serving as the primary tool for digitising manual business processes and replacing Excel-based workflow trackers.
