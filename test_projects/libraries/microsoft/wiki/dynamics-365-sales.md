# Dynamics 365 Sales

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Sales is the CRM application for sales teams in Dynamics 365, providing lead and opportunity management, pipeline forecasting, relationship intelligence, and Copilot-powered AI coaching to help sellers prioritise accounts, draft email communications, and summarise meeting notes. It is the primary Dynamics 365 application for managing the sales cycle from prospecting to deal closure, integrating with LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Outlook, and Teams for seller productivity within existing communication tools.
