# Dynamics 365 Human Resources

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Human Resources is the HR management application in Dynamics 365, covering employee records, leave and absence management, benefits administration, compensation structures, and HR analytics. It integrates with LinkedIn Talent Hub and Azure Active Directory for employee onboarding workflows, and provides managers and employees with self-service HR portals, positioning itself as the HR system of record for organisations that run Dynamics 365 Finance and Supply Chain Management and want a unified HR and ERP data layer in Dataverse.
