# Power Pages

## Purpose

Power Pages is the low-code external-facing web portal platform in Microsoft Power Platform, enabling organisations to publish Dataverse-backed websites accessible to customers, partners, or citizens without custom web development. It supersedes Power Apps Portals, providing templates, a design studio, form and list components bound to Dataverse tables, and built-in authentication supporting Azure AD, social, and local identity providers — serving as the standard approach for self-service portals in Microsoft 365 and Dynamics 365 implementations.
