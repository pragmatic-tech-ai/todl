# Microsoft 365 Archive

## Purpose

Microsoft 365 Archive provides a low-cost, high-volume cold-tier storage layer for SharePoint site content that has passed its active retention window but must be preserved for compliance or legal hold. Rather than deleting or exporting aged content, admins can archive entire SharePoint sites into the 365 Archive tier, where storage costs are significantly lower than active-tier SharePoint, and content remains accessible but with restored-on-demand latency rather than instant access.
