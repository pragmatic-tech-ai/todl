# Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management is the enterprise supply chain and manufacturing ERP application in Dynamics 365, covering procurement, inventory management, warehouse management, transportation logistics, production planning, and quality control for large manufacturers and distributors. It is the cloud-native successor to Dynamics AX supply chain modules, designed for organisations with complex multi-site manufacturing, multi-tier supplier networks, and advanced planning requirements that integrate with IoT sensor data and AI demand forecasting.
