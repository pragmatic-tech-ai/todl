# Dynamics 365 Finance

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Finance is the enterprise financial management application in Dynamics 365, providing general ledger, accounts payable and receivable, budgeting, fixed assets, cash flow forecasting, and global compliance features for large, multi-entity organisations. It is the cloud-native successor to Dynamics AX finance modules, designed for mid-to-large enterprises with complex intercompany, multi-currency, and regulatory reporting requirements that go beyond the capabilities of Business Central.
