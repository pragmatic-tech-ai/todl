# Microsoft Viva Engage

## Purpose

Microsoft Viva Engage is the enterprise social network and community platform in the Microsoft Viva employee experience suite, built on the Yammer service and integrated into Teams and SharePoint. It provides organisation-wide conversation feeds, topic communities, leadership communication channels, and knowledge-crowdsourcing (Questions and Answers), serving as the internal social layer that replaces standalone enterprise social tools and extends employee reach beyond the structured team boundaries of Microsoft Teams channels.
