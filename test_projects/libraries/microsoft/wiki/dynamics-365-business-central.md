# Dynamics 365 Business Central

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Business Central is the all-in-one cloud ERP solution for small and medium-sized businesses, combining financial management, supply chain, project management, sales, and service capabilities in a single application built on the Microsoft Dynamics platform. It is the cloud-native successor to Dynamics NAV, designed for organisations that need a complete business management system without the complexity and infrastructure overhead of enterprise-scale ERP deployments.
