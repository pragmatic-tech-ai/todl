# Microsoft Work IQ

## Purpose

Microsoft Work IQ is an enterprise intelligence capability within the Power Platform that surfaces AI-driven work insights and task intelligence to help knowledge workers and managers understand productivity patterns, surface pending actions, and ground decisions in live business data. It integrates with Microsoft 365 collaboration signals and Dataverse business records to deliver contextual intelligence at the point of work.
