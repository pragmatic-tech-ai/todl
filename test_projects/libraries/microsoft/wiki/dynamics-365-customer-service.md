# Dynamics 365 Customer Service

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Customer Service is the case management and agent-facing service desk application in Dynamics 365, providing a unified workspace for support agents handling inbound requests across email, phone, chat, and social channels. It includes SLA management, knowledge base authoring, routing rules, and Copilot-powered AI assistance that surfaces relevant articles and drafts replies — serving as the primary CRM application for customer support operations that require structured case workflows and escalation paths.
