# Power BI

## Purpose

Power BI is Microsoft's business analytics platform providing interactive data visualisation, self-service BI authoring, AI-powered insights, and enterprise report distribution. It connects to hundreds of data sources, models data in a semantic layer, and publishes dashboards and reports to the Power BI Service for sharing across the organisation. Power BI replaces legacy SSRS and other on-premises reporting tools for most new analytical front-end scenarios and integrates natively with Microsoft Fabric as its BI layer.
