# Dynamics 365 Field Service

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Field Service is the work-order management and dispatch application for organisations that deploy technicians or service crews to customer sites, providing scheduling optimisation, mobile worker apps, asset management, and IoT-triggered maintenance workflows. It connects physical asset monitoring (via Azure IoT Hub) to the CRM service layer, enabling proactive service dispatch when sensor data indicates an imminent failure rather than waiting for a customer-reported breakdown.
