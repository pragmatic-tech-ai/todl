# AI Builder

## Purpose

AI Builder is the no-code and low-code AI capability layer in Microsoft Power Platform, enabling Power Apps and Power Automate authors to embed pre-built and custom AI models — document processing, object detection, sentiment analysis, text classification — directly into business applications and flows without writing ML code. It abstracts Azure AI Services models through a point-and-click model training and consumption interface that surfaces inside the Power Platform studio experience.
