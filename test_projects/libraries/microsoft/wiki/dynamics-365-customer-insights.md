# Dynamics 365 Customer Insights

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Customer Insights is the customer data platform (CDP) in the Dynamics 365 family, unifying customer data from CRM, ERP, e-commerce, and third-party sources into a 360-degree customer profile that drives personalised marketing, sales, and service actions. It consolidates the former Dynamics 365 Customer Insights (profile unification) and Dynamics 365 Marketing (campaign orchestration) under a single product, serving as the single source of customer context for AI-driven segmentation and journey automation across Dynamics 365 applications.
