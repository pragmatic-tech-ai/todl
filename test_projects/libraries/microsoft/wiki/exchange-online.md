# Exchange Online

## Purpose

Exchange Online is the cloud-hosted email and calendaring service in Microsoft 365, providing managed mailboxes, shared calendars, and meeting scheduling without on-premises Exchange Server infrastructure. It serves as the authoritative email backbone for Microsoft 365 tenants and is the primary migration target for organisations retiring on-premises Exchange deployments, offering 99.9% uptime SLA, built-in anti-spam and anti-malware, and integration with Microsoft Teams and Outlook clients.
