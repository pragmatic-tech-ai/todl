# Dynamics 365 Contact Center

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Contact Center is the AI-powered customer service platform that combines voice, digital messaging, and AI-driven agent assistance in a single contact-centre-as-a-service offering. It embeds Copilot AI assistance for live agents, automates common interactions with virtual agents built on Copilot Studio, and routes conversations across voice, chat, email, and social channels — replacing separate CCaaS, telephony, and bot platforms that require manual integration for organisations on the Dynamics 365 customer engagement stack.
