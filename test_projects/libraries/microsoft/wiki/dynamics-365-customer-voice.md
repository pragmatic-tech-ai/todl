# Dynamics 365 Customer Voice

## Purpose

Dynamics 365 Customer Voice is the enterprise feedback management tool in the Dynamics 365 family, enabling organisations to design and send surveys triggered by business events (after a service case closes, post-purchase, post-onboarding) and to automatically link responses back to Dataverse records for analysis within Dynamics 365 dashboards. It replaces standalone survey tools for organisations that need survey responses contextualised against CRM records rather than stored in a separate feedback silo.
